## 🌌 A Different Kind of Practice Room

There is a peculiar moment every improvising musician knows: you are comfortable in three or four keys, and then someone calls a tune in F# major. The room tilts. Your fingers hesitate. The music you *hear* in your head refuses to travel through your hands.

**KeyForge** was born from that tilt.

It is not a metronome with extra steps, nor a scale encyclopedia with a pretty face. It is a **modal mastery engine** — a calm, deliberate space where every one of the twelve tonal centers receives equal respect. It rotates. It transposes. It nudges you gently through the circle until the foreign becomes familiar and the familiar becomes home in twelve different postcodes.

Where other tools shout statistics at you, KeyForge whispers patterns. Where others drown you in options, KeyForge presents a single, well-chosen exercise and asks only that you show up.

> *"The piano has twelve doors. Most of us only ever knock on three."*

This README is intentionally long. If you have landed here from a search for **key trainer for improvisation**, **practice all twelve keys**, **chord change drill tool**, or **scale rotation practice**, you are in exactly the right place.

## ✨ Why Musicians Keep Coming Back

### 🎯 Twelve-Fold Rotation Discipline

KeyForge treats the chromatic circle as a *journey*, not a checklist. Exercises advance by semitone, by circle-of-fifths step, or by user-defined interval leaps. You decide the topography; KeyForge handles the logistics. Practicing a ii–V–I in C? Tomorrow it greets you in F. The day after, Bb. Before you notice, the whole wheel has turned beneath your fingers.

### 🧠 Cognitive Load, Carefully Tuned

There is a fine line between *challenge* and *overwhelm*. KeyForge walks that line intentionally. Each session introduces precisely one variable — a new key, a new chord quality, a new rhythmic subdivision — while keeping everything else stable. This is the pedagogical principle known as **variable isolation**, and it is baked into every drill template.

### 🎼 Chord Progression Laboratory

Stock progressions ship with the app — the canonical ii–V–I, the blues form, the rhythm-changes skeleton, the modal vamp — but the real pleasure is building your own. Chain chords, assign durations, set target keys, and let KeyForge shuffle them into surprising but musically sensible orderings.

### 🎹 Scale & Mode Explorer

Ionian, Dorian, Phrygian, Lydian, Mixolydian, Aeolian, Locrian — plus harmonic and melodic minor, the modes of both, whole-tone, diminished, and the symmetric curiosities that jazz players love to argue about. Each scale is presented relative to the current key so you always know where you *are*, not just where you *could be*.

### 🌍 Multilingual by Default

Musical vocabulary is international; the interface should be too. KeyForge ships with twelve locale packs out of the box, and community translations are welcome. If your language is not yet represented, the string table is a single well-documented file.

### 📱 Responsive on Every Surface

The layout adapts from a 27-inch studio monitor to a phone propped against a music stand. Touch targets swell gracefully; notation reflows; the fullscreen practice view hides every distraction behind a single gesture.

### 🕰️ Around-the-Clock Support

Questions at 3 a.m. before a session? A bug that surfaces only on your particular keyboard? The issue tracker is watched continuously, and response times are measured in hours, not weeks. Support doesn't sleep — or at least, it checks notifications before it does.

### 🧭 Distraction-Free Practice Mode

One exercise. One key. One tempo. That is the entire screen. Everything else — tempo ramps, difficulty controls, statistics — is a deliberate action away.

## 🚀 First Steps Without the Usual Ceremony

You will not find install incantations here. The distribution artifact is self-contained. Retrieve it, launch it, and you are in.

1. Use the macro above to obtain the current release bundle for your platform.
2. Unpack it wherever you prefer to keep small, useful things.
3. Open the application. A brief welcome tour introduces the twelve-key wheel.
4. Choose a starting key — C is the traditional landing pad, but nothing stops you from beginning in Gb.
5. Select a drill: interval, scale, progression, or free improvisation.
6. Press *Begin*. The session timer starts, the metronome breathes, and the wheel begins to turn.

Within one week of five-minute sessions, practitioners report a noticeable reduction in the "key anxiety" that once haunted them.

## 🛠️ Technical Philosophy

- **Local-first.** Your practice data belongs on your device. Sync is opt-in, not assumed.
- **Zero telemetry by default.** No analytics pings, no crash pings, no fingerprinting.
- **Portable formats.** Every user-created artifact — progressions, presets, logs — is stored in plain, human-readable text.
- **Small surface area.** The dependency tree is deliberately shallow so the app remains auditable and long-lived.
- **Accessible foundations.** Keyboard navigation, ARIA labeling, and contrast-conscious palettes are requirements, not afterthoughts.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Is my practice data private?**
Yes. Everything is stored locally unless you explicitly enable sync.

**Does it work without an internet connection?**
Completely. Every asset is bundled.

**Can I contribute a translation?**
Absolutely — the string table is designed for community contribution.

**Which instruments does it support?**
Any. KeyForge is instrument-agnostic; it teaches tonal relationships, not fingerings.

**Is there a mobile version?**
The responsive interface runs beautifully on phones and tablets.

**How long are sessions?**
As short as 60 seconds, as long as you like. Five minutes daily outperforms an hour weekly.

**Can I use it in a classroom?**
Yes, and an educational licensing note is available in the docs folder.

**Will it run on older hardware?**
The minimum footprint is intentionally modest; most machines from the last decade handle it comfortably.
